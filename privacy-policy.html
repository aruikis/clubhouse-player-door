Clubhouse Poker Room — Player Portal

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026

Who runs this

Clubhouse Poker Room is a private home poker league hosted by Anthony Ruikis in Plainfield, Illinois. This policy explains what information the league collects through the Player Portal, and how it is used.

What we collect

When a player signs up on the Player Portal, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Your mobile phone number
  • Your email address (optional)
  • A profile photo (optional)

How we use it

Your information is used only for league administration and for SMS text messages from the Clubhouse Poker Room club number. Those texts cover game-night invitations, schedule updates, and check-in reminders.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Your phone number and email address are visible only to the host. Other players see your name and game stats only. Your information is never sold, rented, or listed anywhere.

Your choices

You can ask the host at any time to update or remove your information. You can also reply STOP to any text to stop receiving texts.

Contact

Players can reach the host through the Player Portal, or by replying to any Clubhouse text.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
