Club Info
House rules & fees

Read these once — tap either note to open it. Done reading? Hide this section for good and it won't show on this device again.

On a $100 league night, $20 of every league player's buy-in goes into the championship pot. That pot pays the points leader's free championship buy-in and boosts the championship prizes. The $20 is part of the price of a league night — and it is not refunded if a player doesn't reach the five nights needed to qualify. No surprises: that's the deal before the first hand is dealt.

Every sit & go takes a flat $5 off the top of the purse — not per player, and never a percentage. So a 10-player, $20 game builds a $200 purse, the house takes its $5, and $195 goes straight into the prizes. That $5 is the Clubhouse's only rake, and it exists for one reason: it covers the real costs of running the room — texting and communication fees (Twilio) for invites, game-night updates, and check-in and recovery codes, server and hosting costs for the league book, this Player Portal, and the TV game display, and Jarvis — Anthony's assistant, who helps run the Clubhouse behind the scenes and is part of what it costs to keep the room going. It's a flat fee to keep the Clubhouse running — not a profit cut, and it never grows with the pot.
